# 1. Record the reader and the pages

## The reader

- **Backdrop:** one plain colour, pulled flat, evenly lit, filling the whole frame behind the reader. Not the same
  colour as their clothes or the book cover.
- **Stand or sit 1 metre (3 feet) in front of it** so there is no shadow on it.
- **Phone on a tripod,** upright or sideways, not moving. The whole reader and the whole book stay inside the frame.
- **Before the reader steps in, take one photo of the empty backdrop from the same spot.** Save it later as
  `my-book/empty_backdrop.jpg`. This one photo makes the cutout much cleaner.
- **Sound:** a quiet room. Read at a natural pace. Chatting about the pictures is fine; it becomes part of the video.
- **Mistakes are fine.** Pause, read the line again, carry on.
- **iPhone:** turn HDR Video off (Settings → Camera → Record Video).

Save the recording as `my-book/reader.mp4` (or `reader.mov`).

## The pages

- Photograph or scan **each page or spread**, straight on, in good even light, with no fingers or glare.
- Crop each picture to the artwork. Landscape pictures (wider than tall) fill the screen best.
- Name them in reading order: `01.jpg`, `02.jpg`, `03.jpg` … and put them in `my-book/pages/`.
